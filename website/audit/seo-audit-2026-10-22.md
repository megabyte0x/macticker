# SEO, AEO and GEO audit, October 22, 2026

## Scope and method

Baseline completed before implementation against the current repository and https://macticker.app. Applied Claude-SEO v2.4.2's `seo-technical`, `seo-geo`, `seo-content`, `seo-schema` and `seo-competitor-pages` checklists from [the reviewed source revision](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo/tree/23a2a811f874361d228fc2681acfc0ad66272736).

Tools: Astro production build, `npm run verify`, the skill's `sitemap_discovery.py` and `agentic_check.py --ua-matrix`, curl, Chromium through agent-browser, and Lighthouse 13.5.0 mobile lab. Competitor claims checked against current primary product listings, not search-result snippets or user reviews. Google's [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) takes precedence over third-party word-count targets or speculative GEO tactics.

## Findings to implement, in priority order

1. **High, accuracy:** stale-data guide says three minutes; `Quote.isStale` in `Sources/Ticker/TokenStore.swift` uses more than 600 seconds (ten minutes). Correct the guide, cite the implementation, and distinguish a failed request from timestamp age.
2. **Medium, dates:** article template displays October 1, 2026 for every article and sets both schema dates to the original date. Derive visible `<time>`, Open Graph and JSON-LD dates from validated publication/update fields. Preserve real publication dates.
3. **Medium, comparison usefulness:** the overview has no comparison matrix and lacks explicit source-review dates. Add a factual matrix, disclose that Megabyte makes Ticker, distinguish published features from hands-on testing, and acknowledge competitor advantages.
4. **Medium, intent coverage:** existing overview does not answer the distinct Coin Tick replacement and Crypto Pro watchlist-versus-portfolio decisions in depth. Add two focused, source-backed comparisons with clear non-fit cases, not duplicated keyword pages.
5. **Medium, attribution:** generic unlinked organization byline gives no editorial context. Add a small about/editorial page, link the real project and publisher, describe source verification and AI assistance without invented credentials or endorsements.
6. **Low, discovery:** add collection/list schema and grouped comparison/setup/data links to the existing guide hub. Related links should remain plain crawlable anchors.
7. **User constraint:** remove homepage article cards and comparison teaser; use one small “Guides & comparisons” footer button. Do not add SEO paragraphs to the landing page or replace its hero.
